S1 - FOUNDING STUDENTS - Call Booked

What starts it: Runs when the OFFICIAL Blueprint Call is booked or rebooked.

What each part does

  1. Write booked and the current appointment ID to the two shared trial fields.
  2. Stop the E2 no-show recovery workflow.
  3. Remove the no-show and canceled tags.
  4. Create or update the same Founding Students opportunity at Call Booked.

S5 - FOUNDING STUDENTS - Blueprint Call Canceled

What starts it: Runs when the OFFICIAL Blueprint Call is canceled.

What each part does

  1. Write canceled and the current appointment ID to the two shared trial fields.
  2. Remove the no-show tag.
  3. Stop E2 no-show recovery.
  4. Send the cancellation email only after an actual cancellation. Calendar contact notices no longer send this email for no-shows.
  5. Leave the pipeline stage unchanged until Alex decides the cancellation-stage rule.

Proof: Published version 4. PASS: real cancellation started S5 v4, wrote canceled, stopped active E2 and delivered the correctly populated cancellation email to Gmail. Calendar Contact cancellation email is disabled to prevent false no-show notices. Alex review remains open.
