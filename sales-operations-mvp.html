3. Alex's SOP, before, during and after the call

When Mia says "I booked you a call"

  1. Open GoHighLevel. Click "Opportunities". Select "SLM Affiliate Lifecycle", then "SLM Partners | Open applications".
  2. Search for the company and primary person's email before adding anything. Open the existing Partner deal. If none exists, Alex or the designated operator creates one at "Potential Affiliate". Do not create a second customer-sales deal for this Partner call.
  3. Record the real appointment, host, meeting link, timezone, primary contact, other attendees, why they are interested, and who introduced them. If it was booked outside GHL, reconcile the existing invite before creating another meeting.
  4. Set Alex as the recruitment deal owner. Create an Alex preparation task due before the call. Check the primary contact owner separately; preserve an existing owner responsible for another active customer relationship.
  5. Mia adds context to the same record. A DM saying "booked" is not the complete handoff.

Day of the call, five minutes

  1. Open the Partner deal, its note and the actual calendar event. Confirm who is attending and who can approve the partnership.
  2. Check communication preferences and any previous refusal. Check approved offer terms. Prepare one relevant campaign idea.
  3. Confirm the meeting link and attendee timezone. GHL task dates were observed in EDT; our reports use Portugal time. Do not assume the displayed clock is Lisbon.

On the call

  1. Learn their audience, relationship with that audience, channels, goals, and approval process. Confirm who would receive the commission and who would approve marketing.
  2. Explain the agreed exchange: what SLM will prepare, what the Partner must approve or distribute, and how attribution and commission work under approved terms.
  3. Ask for a clear outcome: yes, follow-up on an agreed date, not a fit, or do not contact.
  4. If yes, complete the approved signup step and book Mia's confirmed calendar while everyone is present. Invite all required decision-makers to the same meeting. If signup or booking cannot finish, keep an Alex task with a due date. Do not mark the handoff complete.

Within five minutes after the call

  1. Mark the actual appointment outcome, such as "Showed" or "No show". An elapsed meeting time alone does not prove attendance.
  2. Add the handoff note below. Update the Partner deal, not the customer trial appointment fields.
  3. Leave exactly one next action, owner and due date. Keep Alex accountable until Mia explicitly accepts the handoff.
  4. Confirm the invitation reached the primary contact. Additional attendees need their own invitation check. Linking a contact to a deal does not itself invite them.

6. Two people from one company, use this structure
  • Company: one verified business record. Business name and domain help identify it; do not merge companies or people on name alone.
  • Contacts: one record per real person and their own email. Do not put two people's emails on one contact or invent phone numbers.
  • Opportunity, the deal card: one active Partner deal for the company and partnership being discussed. Pick the agreed main point of contact. Add other people through "Additional Contacts" if available in this account.
  • Appointment: one event with the primary attendee and other attendees as guests. Check invitation delivery separately. If an additional-contact control is unavailable, use separate contacts plus company association and linked notes. Do not create a duplicate deal as a workaround.
  • Roles: record decision-maker, marketing contact, finance/payee contact and campaign approver in the note. One person may have several roles.
  • Automation: pipeline writers should run for "Contact only", not each guest. Current AF3 uses Contact only, but matches by primary contact, not verified company/deal ID. A colleague making a separate booking can still create a separate deal. Until repaired, use the same primary contact for the booking and check for an existing company deal.

Required duplicate and stop rules

Target behavior below. Only the dated test results certify implementation; complete trial, reply, payment and simultaneous-event handling remain open.
